01 // Overview

Introduction

ZONIC ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use the Zonic iOS application.

By using Zonic, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

02 // Data Collection

Data We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide and improve our services:

  • Location Data — Precise GPS location to track your running routes and distance.
  • Fitness Data — Distance, speed, pace, and activity duration.
  • Account Information — Name, username, and email address when you register.
  • Leaderboard Scores — Running statistics (distance, time) shared on global, national, and city leaderboards.
  • Usage Data — App interactions, features used, and session data for analytics.
  • Device Identifiers — Device ID for fraud prevention and multi-account detection.
03 // Leaderboard

Global Leaderboard & Consent

Your consent is required. Before your scores are published on the global, Uzbekistan, or city leaderboard, we will ask for your explicit permission. You may opt out at any time from your profile settings.

When you participate in the leaderboard, your username and running statistics (distance covered) are visible to other users. No sensitive personal information is displayed publicly.

04 // Usage

How We Use Your Data

  • To track and display your running activity and progress.
  • To power real-time global, national, and city leaderboards.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity (e.g., driving instead of running).
  • To improve app performance and user experience.
  • To send important service notifications.
  • To enable marketplace features and rewards (XP system).
05 // Sharing

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers — Third-party services that help operate our platform (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics).
  • Other Users — Only your username and leaderboard score (with your consent).
  • Legal Authorities — When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
06 // Retention

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

07 // Your Rights

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for leaderboard participation at any time.
  • Opt out of analytics data collection.
08 // Security

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

09 // Children

Children's Privacy

Zonic is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.
